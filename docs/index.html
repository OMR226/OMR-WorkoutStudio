FAQ

Common questions

Which edition should I download?

Choose Lite 1.0 for the workout-video creation workflow. Choose Pro 2.0 if you also want Live Workout, progressive overload, mobile tracking, rep capture, recording and history.

Is Pro a replacement for Lite?

Pro includes the video-generation capabilities of Lite and adds interactive training features. Lite remains available as the simpler video-focused edition.

Does the app upload my workout plan or videos?

The current desktop editions are local-first. Files remain on your machine unless you explicitly use a sharing/upload workflow.

Can Pro be used from a phone?

Yes. Pro includes the mobile Live Workout companion/PWA workflow. The Windows app remains the primary planner and video-rendering host.

Can I upload generated videos to YouTube?

Yes, but music rights remain your responsibility. Use properly licensed tracks, the official YouTube Audio Library, or beeps-only mode when appropriate.
